About the College of Science

The University of Rizal System - Morong Campus College of Science is a dynamic academic hub that cultivates curiosity, critical thinking, and innovation through a diverse range of scientific disciplines. Offering five distinct yet interconnected programs, the college is committed to developing students into professionals equipped to solve real-world problems, contribute to scientific advancement, and make a lasting impact on society.
