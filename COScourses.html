Programs Offered

Bachelor of Science in Biology
Major in Microbiology - Prof. Alana Buenviaje

The Bachelor of Science in Biology Major in Microbiology is an undergraduate program offered at the University of Rizal System (URS) Morong Campus under the College of Science. This program is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of microorganisms and their impact on various biological systems.

​ Students enrolled in this program will engage in both theoretical and practical coursework, covering topics such as microbial physiology, genetics, ecology, and laboratory techniques. The curriculum aims to equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary for careers in research, healthcare, environmental science, and biotechnology.
