Dean of College of Industrial Technology

Dr. Rommel R. Castro

     Prof. Frederick R. Feliciano is the Dean of the College of Industrial Technology at the University of Rizal System (URS) Morong Campus. In addition to his role as Dean, he has served as the Head of Admissions at URS Morong.
     Prof. Feliciano's leadership is instrumental in overseeing the development and implementation of academic programs within the College of Industrial Technology. His dedication to academic excellence and student success contributes significantly to the college's mission of providing quality education and preparing students for careers in industrial technology.
